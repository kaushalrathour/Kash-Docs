# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: May 2026_

## Overview

Kash is an offline-first personal finance application designed to minimize data collection and maximize user privacy.

Most financial data remains on your device and is processed locally.

This Privacy Policy explains:

- what information Kash collects
- how that information is used
- what is never collected
- how optional cloud features work

By using Kash, you agree to this policy.

---

# Information We Collect

## 1. Analytics & Diagnostic Data

Kash may collect limited analytics and diagnostic information to improve app reliability and user experience.

This may include:

- app version
- operating system version
- device model
- crash reports
- feature usage frequency
- parser success/failure rates
- session activity

This data does **NOT** include:

- transaction amounts
- merchant names
- transaction descriptions
- account balances
- budgets
- personal financial records

Analytics can be disabled from:

`Settings → Privacy`

---

## 2. Financial Data

By default, your financial data is stored locally on your device.

This includes:

- transactions
- budgets
- accounts
- analytics history
- categories
- notes

Kash does not upload this data to our servers unless optional sync features are enabled.

---

## 3. AI Features

Kash may use AI-powered language processing for certain assistant features.

Examples:

- natural language understanding
- query interpretation
- intent extraction

Whenever possible:

- parsing is handled locally
- deterministic config rules are preferred over AI calls

Kash does not use your financial data for advertising or profiling.

AI providers may temporarily process requests required to fulfill assistant functionality.

---

# What We Never Collect

Kash never intentionally collects:

- banking credentials
- credit/debit card numbers
- UPI PINs
- passwords
- government IDs
- full financial histories for advertising

Kash does not sell user data.

---

# Optional Cloud Sync

Cloud sync may be introduced as an optional feature.

If enabled:

- your data is encrypted before upload
- servers store encrypted data only
- encryption keys remain controlled by the user

Kash cannot read encrypted financial records.

---

# Third-Party Services

Kash may use third-party services for:

- crash reporting
- analytics
- notifications
- infrastructure

Examples may include:

- Sentry
- AppsFlyer
- OneSignal
- Firebase

These services may collect limited technical metadata required for functionality.

---

# Data Retention

Most financial data remains on-device until deleted by the user.

Diagnostic and analytics data may be retained for a limited period for stability and abuse prevention.

---

# Your Rights

You may:

- disable analytics
- delete app data locally
- uninstall the application
- request account deletion (if cloud accounts exist)

---

# Children’s Privacy

Kash is not directed toward children under 13.
