# Account & Data Deletion

_Last updated: May 2026_

# Local Data

Kash stores most data locally on your device.

You can remove local data by:

1. Opening Kash  
2. Going to Settings  
3. Selecting **Reset / Delete Data**

Uninstalling the app may also remove locally stored data depending on your platform.

---

# Cloud Accounts

If cloud sync or online accounts are enabled in the future, users may request account deletion.

To request deletion:

**Email:** kaushal@appmatters.in

Include:

- your account email
- deletion request details

We will process deletion requests within a reasonable timeframe.

---

# What Gets Deleted

When applicable:

- account information
- encrypted cloud backups
- associated metadata

may be permanently deleted.

---

# Important

If end-to-end encrypted backups are used:

- losing your recovery phrase may permanently prevent recovery
- deleted data may not be recoverable
